About Slageng

Welcome to Slageng

The premier high-end urban grill and entertainment hub located in the heart of Seshego. We successfully blend the rich, authentic spirit of a traditional South African kasi establishment with the sleek sophistication of an upmarket modern lounge.
Slageng is far more than a standard nightclub; we operate as a full-force restaurant and premium butcher where the local community gathers to celebrate life. Our signature hybrid model allows you to select premium cuts directly from our butcher and braai for free, delivering genuine local flavour in a high-energy, stylish setting.
The Slageng ExperienceEvery week, we curate an unmatched entertainment calendar designed to help you break away from the daily grind and step into the hottest groove in Limpopo. Our weekly rhythm builds an unforgettable weekend experience from the ground up. We kick off with Rare Fridays, bringing you high-calibre national headliners and premium urban nightlife.
The energy carries through our high-fashion Saturdays, leading directly into our legendary Famous Sundays, where we welcome the upcoming week with deep, soulful sets and high-spirited fun. Whether you are looking for an exclusive VIP night out or a relaxed afternoon with friends, our dance floors offer a carefree, judgment-free escape where you can simply live in the moment.
Our Core Mission At the center of our brand is a deep commitment to our roots and a promise that priority is always provided to local talent. Slageng serves as a proud launching pad for rising regional DJs, producers, and vocalists, sharing the stage alongside South Africa’s biggest musical giants. We believe in fostering a safe, vibrant space where local culture thrives and home-grown artists can shine.
